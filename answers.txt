Calculating the averages is only a groupBy and average aggregation, so it
appears to take less time than reading and parsing the JSON input. The cached
version took 42.249 seconds, which was slightly slower overall than the
schema-without-cache version in this run. This small difference can be caused
by normal timing variation and the cost of placing the DataFrame into the
cache. However, caching still prevents Spark from recalculating the averages
from the original input when producing the second sorted output.

3. I used .cache() exactly once: on the pagecounts DataFrame after it had
   been filtered (English pages only, no Main_Page, no Special:*) and cut
   down to the (hour, title, views) columns -- i.e. right before the
   groupBy. That DataFrame is used twice: once to compute the maximum view
   count per hour, and again when the per-hour maximums are joined back to
   it to pick out the most-viewed page(s). Caching it means the input is
   only read and filtered once instead of twice.
